Terms and Conditions

Please read the following terms carefully before creating your insurance policy

1. Policy Coverage

Your insurance policy provides coverage as specified in your policy documents. Coverage begins on the policy start date after approval and continues until the policy end date, subject to timely premium payments.

2. Premium Payments

Monthly premium payments are due on the policy anniversary date each month. Failure to pay premiums may result in policy cancellation. Premium amounts are calculated based on your selected coverage amount and policy type.

3. Policy Approval

All policies are subject to approval by our underwriting team. We reserve the right to decline coverage based on our risk assessment criteria. You will be notified of the approval status within 5 business days.

4. Cancellation

You may cancel your policy at any time by providing 30 days written notice. Refunds for unused premiums will be calculated on a pro-rata basis, minus any applicable cancellation fees as outlined in your policy documents.

5. Claims Process

Claims must be submitted through our online portal within 30 days of the incident. All claims are subject to investigation and approval. Approved claims will be processed according to the terms specified in your policy coverage.

6. Data Protection

Your personal information is protected in accordance with applicable data protection laws. We use your data solely for policy administration, claims processing, and customer service purposes.

By creating a policy with SecureGuard Insurance, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by these terms and conditions. If you have any questions, please contact our customer service team before proceeding.
